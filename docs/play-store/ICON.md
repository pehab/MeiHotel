Prompt: Transform the MeiTüftler brand mascot illustration into its hotel-manager sibling. Preserve the vivid turquoise luminous background, cheerful glossy yellow round mascot, large warm dark oval eyes, smile, thick dark-brown outlines and golden cartoon highlights. Replace tinkerer goggles, marble ramp and gear with a teal bellhop cap with gold band, teal vest, welcoming raised hand, shiny gold reception bell and wooden reception desk. Add a small cream hotel with terracotta roof and lit windows. Make the face dominant and legible at launcher size. Restrained sparkles, no text or watermark, square full bleed composition with central launcher-safe framing.
